# DocIntel Notes, AI credits and templates audit

**Audit date:** 22 October 2026. **Scope:** checked-in backend and frontend, local configuration, deterministic tests, and previously published aggregate usage evidence. This is an audit, not approval to change billing or deploy features. No application code, schema, active allowance, balance, subscription, Paystack record, or paid provider request was changed.

## 1. Executive summary

- **Workload credits are implemented but dormant in the checked-in defaults and this checkout.** `config/ai_credits.php:9` defaults `DOCINTEL_AI_CREDITS_ENABLED` to false; the local `.env` has no override. Production effective environment was not inspected. Under the enabled contract, the customer pays the immutable **quoted band**, once on a delivered Ready result; lower actual Anthropic usage does not reduce that fixed charge. That is not an accounting overcharge if the quote and outcome meet the agreed terms. It can still be poor value, especially for dense files and conservative synthesis estimates.
- **The quote can jump sharply for reasons that are not measured customer workload.** Every XLS/XLSX/CSV is dense regardless of content (`ExtractionCapacity::density`, lines 79–102); dense shifts one band (`QuoteService::classify`, lines 64–80). A 20k-token ordinary document would be 10 credits by token band, but the documented USD 0.50 synthesis reservation raises it to 30; a 60k document rises from 30 to 80. The reservation is a bound for fallback and repair, not realized cost. The deterministic `--what-if` result confirms these examples, not their incidence in production.
- **Economic sustainability of a substantially larger headline allowance is unproved.** The latest checked-in aggregate is historical, sparse, and mostly missing cost. The live local reporting database was unavailable. At full redemption and the configured USD 0.027 provider cap per credit, even a 50% allowance increase drives monthly after-fee modeled margin below the provisional 70% target. Do not publish numerical new allowances until complete Ready-operation cost distributions and invoice reconciliation exist.
- **Personal Notes API is substantive; the frontend is a plain-text minimum.** Create/list/edit/delete and quoted `If-Match` delete are wired. Rich notes already stored by the backend cannot be edited by this pane: it sends `body` alone and the backend explicitly returns 422 for a rich note. Drafts have no close/navigation protection; citations have snapshots but no click-through to their passage. These are user-visible gaps even though focused tests pass.
- **The three named templates are operational on-screen dashboard compositions, with evidence and missing-data handling.** Switching is local state/localStorage and makes no AI call. They are not three report export formats: Word export is one generic builder that ignores the selected template and saved Notes. The checked-in production frontend env sets `VITE_DOCUMENT_WORKSPACE=0`, hiding the whole workspace UI unless overridden at deployment; actual deployment state is unverified.

### Contract versus possible customer overcharge

**No proved duplicate debit under the enabled contract:** the 90 focused backend tests include quote immutability, concurrent funding, failure release, OCR settlement, provider-attempt holds and Notes concurrency. Code uses workspace locks and one current `billing_operations` row per kind/resource. This supports, but does not prove, production-wide idempotency. A new explicit full reanalysis is a separately quoted customer request; an internal queue retry is not.

**Poor value without a billing violation:** a delivered Ready result settles the fixed accepted quote even if actual provider cost is much lower (`CreditAccountant::settle`). The product must explain that credits price a workload category and result, not pass-through token usage. Unnecessary band shifts, inadequate result quality, hidden OCR addition, or charging a partial result contrary to stated terms would be contract/fairness defects; their production incidence is unmeasured.

**Cap caveat:** `OperationSpend::reserveAttempt` prevents estimated maximum requests from exceeding the cap, and unknown dispatched calls keep their hold. `finishAttempt` accepts the provider's actual cost without enforcing `actual <= reserved` (`OperationSpend.php:154-175`). A response above the estimate can therefore exceed the nominal operation ceiling; concurrent in-flight responses may make the overrun larger than one response. This is a provider-margin risk, not an extra customer debit. Reconcile with actual attempts before describing the cap as absolute.

## 5. Credit economics and allowance scenarios

**Checked-in modeled assumptions:** USD 0.027 maximum provider cost per credit, 129.79 KES/USD, 2.9% payment fee, 70% target (`config/ai_credits.php:25-30`); KES **3.50433** provider ceiling per redeemed credit. These are configuration assumptions, not measured cost or current external FX/payment pricing. Full-redemption margin = `(plan revenue × 0.971 − allowance × 3.50433) / plan revenue`. Provider spend is bounded by the modeled cap only to the extent the attempt gate enforces it; see C3. Infrastructure, tax, refunds, support, acquisition and free-user grants are excluded.

| Illustrative allowance scenario (Starter / Professional per month) | Starter monthly margin | Professional monthly margin | Starter annual monthly-equivalent margin | Professional annual monthly-equivalent margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current defaults **100 / 250** | 73.7% | 72.1% | 69.1% | 67.1% |
| Moderate illustration **150 / 375** | 62.1% | 59.6% | 55.0% | 52.0% |
| Substantial illustration **250 / 625** | 38.7% | 34.5% | 27.0% | 22.0% |

These two larger schedules are **stress illustrations, not proposed final allowances**. At the same cap, the 70% after-fee mathematical limits are about 116/270 credits monthly and 96/225 annual monthly-equivalent; they leave no safety margin for cap overrun or other costs. A lower per-document credit charge raises documents possible per plan but does not change the full-redemption ceiling if customers use every credit. A proportional increase in both allowance and all band prices changes the headline only, not customer value. A genuine fairer schedule must lower charges for demonstrably low-cost/low-workload categories while retaining sufficient cap for useful completion, then test mix and redemption.

| Workload example, deterministic config | Current quote / provider cap | Effective documents per full Starter / Professional allowance | Evidence limit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 5k ordinary text tokens | 4 / USD 0.108 | 25 / 62 | Token estimate and cap, not completed cost. |
| 20k ordinary incremental tokens, USD 0.50 synthesis hold | 30 / USD 0.810 | 3 / 8 | Reservation drives band; actual may be lower. |
| 60k ordinary incremental tokens, USD 0.85 hold | 80 / USD 2.160 | 1 / 3 | Reservation drives band; no measured completion rate. |
| One-page OCR plus simple analysis | 20 + 4 = 24; OCR USD 0.020 + analysis USD 0.108 | 4 / 10 | OCR cap is page based; may be followed by a larger analysis quote. |
| AI comparison | 12 / USD 0.324 | 8 / 20 | No completed-cost sample available. |

`cost per credit` by actual category is **unavailable**; the table gives ceilings: document and comparison USD 0.027/credit, one-page OCR USD 0.001/credit, 60-page OCR USD 0.020/credit. Quoted-versus-actual cost, effective credits per **observed completed** document, missing cost by band, and p50/p75/p90/p95 costs cannot be calculated responsibly from the accessible data. The older five fully known document costs are four incomplete and one Ready, and 53% of runs lacked cost; a percentile from them would give false precision.

**Measurement gate before any allowance decision:** obtain aggregate-only, read-only operation rows for a defined post-fix period: quote version/band/credits/preflight tokens/density/route/OCR pages, Ready or partial state, revision, charged/refunded amount, all attributed provider attempts and known/uncertain USD, and coverage/quality outcome. Report n and missing-cost fraction for each band × route × dense × OCR cohort, separately for comparison/Q&A/reanalysis and free/paid/annual users. Join attempts without double-counting run rows, reconcile totals to Anthropic invoice and payment fees, measure p50/p75/p90/p95 plus tail and full-redemption utilization, and stress FX/model pricing. Require a useful Ready result under each cap at p95 (or revise/refuse that band). Do not query document text, prompts, names, identities or payment details.

## 8. Confirmed defects versus unverified risks

**Confirmed by code/contract and deterministic tests:** dense type shift and reservation-driven quote jumps (C1); fixed quoted settlement rather than usage rebate (contract behavior); economics report omissions (C2); failed-reanalysis immediate release guard (C7, code path confirmed, production incidence unknown); backend rich-note/body-only frontend incompatibility (N1); absent dirty guard/navigation (N2/N3); selected-template/export disconnect (T1); checked-in production workspace flag off. Current backend feature tests pass, which supports the positive paths but does not erase these uncovered gaps.

**Unverified production risks:** effective production flags/prices; incidence of excessive quoted bands; cap overrun or orphan holds in real traffic (C3/C6); actual ledger/display divergence (C5); OCR-late-admission frequency (C4); browser-level Notes usability; provider invoice mismatch; profitable larger allowances.
